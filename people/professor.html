Biography

Sung-Yun Park received the B.S. degree in electrical engineering from Pusan National University, Busan, South Korea, in 2005 and the M.S. degree in electrical engineering from the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), Daejeon, South Korea, in 2008, where he conducted research on photonic devices. From 2008 to 2010, he was a Member of Technical Staff at Fairchild Semiconductor (currently, On-Semiconductor), Bucheon, South Korea, where he worked on high-voltage mixed-signal circuit design. He subsequently continued his studies in integrated circuits, receiving the M.Eng. degree in electrical and computer engineering from Cornell University, Ithaca, NY, USA, in 2011 and the Ph.D. degree in electrical engineering and computer science from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI, USA, in 2016. From 2016 to 2018, he was a Research Fellow at the University of Michigan, where he served as an Assistant Research Scientist from 2018 to 2019. In 2019, he joined the Department of Electronics Engineering at Pusan National University as an Assistant Professor. Since 2024, he has been an Associate Professor. His research interests include integrated circuits, high-voltage mixed-signal circuit design, and bioelectronic and neural interface systems.
